Support

TeleGuard lets two people on the same Wi-Fi or Bluetooth range open a private conversation by scanning a QR code. Messages are encrypted end-to-end and stay on the devices.

Having trouble?

  • Make sure both phones are on the same Wi-Fi network and Bluetooth is on.
  • Allow Camera access to scan a QR code, and Local Network access to connect.
  • Both people must have TeleGuard open at the same time — there is no offline delivery.
  • If a connection times out, move the phones closer and try again.
