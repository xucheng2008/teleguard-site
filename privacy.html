Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

TeleGuard is designed so that your conversations never reach a server. This policy explains exactly what the app does with your data.

1. What we collect

Nothing. TeleGuard has no account system, no analytics, no advertising SDKs, and no backend. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data.

2. What stays on your device

  • Identity keys are generated on first launch and stored in the iOS Keychain.
  • Messages are encrypted with AES-GCM and stored locally (SwiftData) on your device.
  • Contact identity (the person you scanned) is stored locally so you can verify it later.

Deleting the app removes this local data. Because there is no backup service, deleted messages cannot be recovered.

3. Permissions we ask for

  • Camera — only to scan a QR code. Frames are processed on-device and never uploaded.
  • Local Network — to connect directly to the other phone over Wi-Fi/Bluetooth. Only connection metadata needed to establish the link is exchanged.

4. Message delivery

Messages travel directly between the two devices over the local network and are encrypted end-to-end (X25519 key exchange, HKDF, ChaCha20-Poly1305). Apple, our team, or any third party cannot read them. Messages are not stored on any server, so both people must be online at the same time.

5. Children

TeleGuard does not collect data from anyone, including children under 13.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with an updated date.
